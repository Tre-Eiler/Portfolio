Companies present in a vendor platform but not mapped to an auto-reconciled PSA agreement addition. Unmapped does not mean unbilled — many are invoiced manually. Read these as items to verify, not confirmed leakage. Products with no integration are shown too. That is a visibility gap, not a to-do: the MSP may not sell the product at all, so the reconciliation gap there could be zero — we simply cannot tell.
